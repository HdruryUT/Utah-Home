How the scores and estimates work
Where listings come from
A scan every morning reads Century21's public single-family pages, which carry the local MLS listings, for each Utah County city. It then opens each new listing to confirm its type, lot, year built and description. Zillow, Homes.com, UtahRealEstate.com and KSL are searched as cross-checks. A brand-new listing can take a day to appear, so always confirm details on the listing link.
What's filtered out
Townhomes, condos, twin homes, homes on lots under 0.05 acres (attached homes labeled single family), land leases, 55+ communities, auctions, fewer than 2 bedrooms, and anything over $395k.
Fit score
Price vs budget (20), price per sqft vs the city's going rate (25), beds and baths (15), lot and garage (10), condition (10), return upside (15), no HOA (3), time on market (2). Dealbreakers subtract points: cash offers only (−15), short sale (−6), under contract (−5), may not qualify for FHA (−4).
After-repair value
The higher of (a) size × the city's median closed $/sqft for homes under $500k, scaled up for smaller-than-typical homes, plus 12% for a renovated condition, and (b) asking price + rehab cost × a return multiple (1.6 heavy, 1.3 medium, 1.0 light). Capped at 1.6× asking price.
Rehab estimate
Light $15/sqft (paint, flooring), medium $35/sqft (kitchen, bath, systems), heavy $60/sqft (as-is or fixer). The tier comes from the listing's wording and year built.
Quick flip
After-repair value − price − rehab − 7% selling costs − 3% buying and holding costs.
Fix up and live 5 years
After-repair value grown 3.5% a year for 5 years, minus price, rehab, and 7% selling costs at the end. Mortgage interest and the rent you'd otherwise pay aren't counted.
Treat these as estimates
These are ballpark figures to help you compare homes. Get an inspection and a contractor's bid before relying on any of them. Photos and listing details belong to the listing brokers.
